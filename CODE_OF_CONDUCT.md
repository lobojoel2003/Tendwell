# Code of conduct

Tendwell exists to help people through grief. The way we treat each other here should reflect that.

## What we ask

- **Be kind and patient.** Some people who open issues are writing on the worst week of their lives.
  Thank them, and answer gently.
- **Assume good intent.** Ask before you conclude.
- **Respect experience.** People grieve differently and come from different cultures, faiths and
  family shapes. There's no single right way.
- **Keep it on topic and private.** Don't share anyone's personal details, including your own, in
  public issues, screenshots or examples.
- **Disagree about ideas, not people.**

## What isn't acceptable

Harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual language or imagery, sharing someone's private
information, or pressuring anyone, including grieving people, to buy, sign up for or believe
anything.

## If something goes wrong

Contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub security advisories](https://github.com/lobojoel2003/Tendwell/security/advisories/new) or by
messaging [@lobojoel2003](https://github.com/lobojoel2003). Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and block people who don't follow this code.

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you
represent the project.
