Pets often notice a loss too. A familiar person and a normal routine can help them settle.

1. **Arrange short-term care.** Ask someone to feed and walk them, or to take them home for a while.
   Neighbors often already know the animals.
2. **Gather what they need.** Food, medicine, leashes, carriers, beds and any vet records.
3. **Look for wishes.** Some people name a caregiver for their pets in their will or leave a note.
   A few set up a pet trust.
4. **Tell their vet.** The vet can share medical history and may know of anyone the person asked to
   look after their animals.
5. **Decide on a long-term home when you're ready.** If no one in the family can keep them, a local
   rescue or the breeder may be able to help find a good home.

It's okay if no one can take them permanently. Finding them a caring new home is a kind thing to do.
