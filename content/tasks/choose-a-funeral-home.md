You don't need to have everything decided before you call. The first conversation is usually just
about bringing your loved one into their care.

1. **Check for wishes or a prepaid plan.** Look for a note, a will, or papers from a funeral home.
   Some people pay for their funeral in advance. If they wanted to donate their body to science,
   contact that program first.
2. **Ask for prices.** A federal rule called the Funeral Rule means funeral homes must give you price
   information over the phone and an itemized price list when you visit. You can choose only the
   items you want.
3. **Know you have choices.** You can buy a casket or urn elsewhere, and the funeral home cannot
   refuse it or charge a fee for using it.
4. **Bring the basics.** They will ask for details for the death certificate, such as full name,
   date and place of birth, Social Security number, parents' names and education. If you don't
   know something, it's okay to say so.

It's okay to ask a friend to come with you, take notes, or make some of the calls. If a price
feels high, it's okay to ask for a simpler option or to call another funeral home.
