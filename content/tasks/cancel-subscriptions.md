These are often small amounts, but they add up and can keep going for years. Work through your
Accounts list one at a time.

1. **Find them.** Recurring charges on bank and card statements are the best clue. Common ones are
   streaming services, phone plans, newspapers and magazines, gyms, clubs, meal kits, software, cloud
   storage, app store subscriptions and regular charity donations.
2. **Cancel online or by phone where you can.** Many companies have a bereavement page on their
   website, often found by searching their help pages for "deceased" or "bereavement".
3. **Use a letter when you need one.** Some companies want it in writing, with a copy of the death
   certificate.
4. **Ask for a refund** of any time already paid for. Many companies will give one.
5. **Think twice before cancelling something others use.** A shared streaming account or phone plan
   may be easier to transfer to someone in the family.
6. **Watch the next statement.** Make sure the charges have stopped.

It's okay to keep something running for a while if it brings comfort, like their favorite radio
subscription or a photo storage account you haven't looked through yet.
