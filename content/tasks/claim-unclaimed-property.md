States hold billions of dollars in unclaimed property, waiting for owners or their families. It's a
small task that sometimes turns up a pleasant surprise.

1. **Search their name** on MissingMoney.com, a free search run with the state unclaimed property
   offices. Also search the official unclaimed property website of each state they lived in.
2. **Try variations.** Maiden names, middle initials and old addresses can all turn up different
   results.
3. **Never pay to search.** Official state searches are free. Be wary of companies that offer to
   find money for a fee.
4. **Make a claim.** Each state has its own claim form. You'll usually need a death certificate and
   proof that you are the executor or an heir.
5. **Search again later.** New property is reported every year, so it's worth checking once more
   before the estate is closed.

It's okay to search for yourself and family members while you're there. Many people find something.
