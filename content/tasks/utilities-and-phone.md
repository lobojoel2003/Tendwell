Utilities often need a little thought rather than a quick cancellation, because the home may still
need heat, light and water for a while.

1. **List the services.** Electricity, gas, water, trash, internet, cable or TV, home phone and their
   cell phone.
2. **If someone still lives there,** ask each company to transfer the account into their name.
3. **If the home is empty,** keep electricity, heat and water on until the home is sold, rented out
   or handed back. A cold, empty home can have frozen pipes, and some insurance needs the home to be
   kept up.
4. **Read the meters** on the day you call, if you can, so the final bill is accurate.
5. **Their cell phone.** Before cancelling, think about whether you need it for two-step
   verification codes for their accounts. Some families keep the number for a few months.
6. **Final bills** are usually paid by the estate. If an account was in the person's name alone,
   family members aren't usually responsible for it personally.

It's okay to leave this until the other big steps are done, as long as the bills are covered.
