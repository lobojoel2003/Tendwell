Most people have more accounts than anyone realizes. You will keep finding them for a while, and
that's normal. The **Accounts** list in your plan is a good place to keep track, and others in your
family can add to it too.

1. **Look at bank and card statements.** Regular payments point to subscriptions, memberships,
   insurance and utilities.
2. **Look through the mail and email.** Bills, renewal notices and receipts arrive for weeks.
3. **Check their wallet and phone.** Cards, apps and saved passwords can show accounts you didn't
   know about.
4. **Note the basics for each one.** The name, what it is, and who is handling it. If you write down
   an account number, the last four digits are usually enough.
5. **Close things one at a time, later.** Some accounts need to stay open for a while, such as the
   electricity in a home that is being sold.

It's okay to add things to the list as they turn up over the coming months.
