---
title: Keep their home and belongings safe
phase: first-days
order: 60
summary: >-
  If their home will be empty for a while, a few simple steps can protect it and the things inside.
effort: hour
regions: [all]
---

An empty home can feel strange to walk into. You don't need to sort anything yet, just keep it safe.

1. **Lock up.** Close windows, lock the doors and keep the keys in one place. Make a note of who
   else has a key.
2. **Look after valuables and papers.** Put jewelry, cash, checkbooks and important documents
   somewhere secure. Don't give anything away yet; the will or the executor will decide later.
3. **Deal with food and plants.** Empty the fridge of anything that will spoil and water any plants,
   or ask a neighbor to.
4. **Keep it looking lived in.** Lights on a timer and collecting the mail help. Some families ask
   someone to stay at the home during the funeral, as homes can be targeted when the date is public.
5. **Keep heat on in cold weather.** A low setting helps stop pipes from freezing.
6. **Look after any vehicles.** Keep them locked and somewhere safe, and keep the keys.

It's okay to ask a neighbor or friend to keep an eye on the place. Most people are glad to help in
a concrete way.
