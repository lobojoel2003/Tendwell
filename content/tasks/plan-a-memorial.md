Remembering someone can take many forms, and there is no deadline. Some families mark the first
anniversary; others choose a date that meant something to the person.

1. **A headstone or marker.** Cemeteries often have rules about size and materials, so ask for them
   before you order. Many families wait several months before a stone is placed.
2. **For veterans,** the VA may provide a headstone, marker or medallion at no cost.
3. **A gathering.** A celebration of life, a memorial service or a simple meal can happen at any
   time, and it can be as small or large as you like.
4. **Something lasting.** A tree, a bench, a scholarship, a donation to a cause they cared about, or
   a book of memories from friends and family.
5. **Share the planning.** This can be a lovely task for someone who wants to help but isn't
   handling the paperwork.

It's okay if your family remembers them in different ways. More than one kind of memorial is
perfectly fine.
