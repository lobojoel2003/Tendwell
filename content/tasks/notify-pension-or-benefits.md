Social Security is a separate step. This one is for anything else they were receiving, like a
workplace pension, an annuity or benefits from another government program.

1. **Make a list of what they received.** Bank statements show regular deposits. Common ones are
   workplace pensions, federal or state retirement pensions, military retirement pay, Railroad
   Retirement, annuities, and programs like Medicaid or food assistance.
2. **Contact each one.** Tell them about the death and ask:
   - whether a payment for the month of death, or after it, needs to be returned
   - whether a spouse or other family member can receive a **survivor pension**
   - what they need from you, such as a death certificate
3. **Keep their bank account open for now.** Payments sometimes need to be returned from it.
4. **Write down who you spoke to and when.** It helps if questions come up later.

It's okay if you don't know all their benefits. Their mail and bank statements over the next few
months will show you.
