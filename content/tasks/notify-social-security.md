In most cases, the funeral home reports the death to the Social Security Administration (SSA) when
you give them the person's Social Security number. You may not need to do anything, but it's good
to confirm.

1. **Ask the funeral director** whether they reported the death to Social Security.
2. **If they didn't**, call Social Security at 1-800-772-1213 (TTY 1-800-325-0778) or visit a local
   office.
3. **If they were receiving benefits**, the payment for the month they died, and any later months,
   usually has to be returned. Payments arrive a month behind, so this is often the payment that
   arrives the month after the death.
   - If it was paid by direct deposit, tell the bank. The bank will usually return it to Social
     Security.
   - If it came as a check, don't cash it.
4. **Ask about survivors benefits** while you are on the phone. A spouse, young children or other
   family members may be able to receive payments. There is a separate step for this.

It's okay to ask them to repeat things or to send information in writing. They talk to grieving
families every day.
