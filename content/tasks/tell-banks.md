Most banks have a bereavement or estates team. You can call, visit a branch or send a letter. They
will explain what they need from you.

1. **Let them know about the death.** They will usually freeze accounts that were only in the
   person's name. This protects the money until the right person can deal with it.
2. **Ask what happens to each account:**
   - **Joint accounts** usually pass to the other account holder.
   - Accounts with a **payable-on-death (POD)** beneficiary usually go to that person.
   - Accounts only in their name usually become part of the estate.
3. **Ask about regular payments.** Some may need to stop, and some, like a mortgage, may need to keep
   going for now.
4. **Send a copy of the death certificate** if they ask for one.

Try not to use money from an account that was only in their name unless you are the executor, or
the bank tells you it's allowed. Even with good intentions, it can cause problems later.

It's okay to tell the bank you don't know all the details yet. You can come back to them once an
executor is in place.
