Cars are handled by each state's motor vehicle agency (often called the DMV), and the rules differ
from state to state.

1. **Keep it insured and safe.** Don't let anyone drive it unless they are covered by insurance.
   Tell the insurer about the death, and ask how to keep cover in place for now.
2. **Find the title.** It shows who owns the car. Some states let owners name a transfer-on-death
   beneficiary on the title.
3. **Check for a loan.** If money is still owed, the lender has a claim on the car. Contact them to
   ask about options.
4. **Ask your state's DMV how to transfer it.** Usually you'll need the title, a death certificate
   and either the court's paperwork or a small estate form.
5. **Return or transfer the plates** and cancel the registration if your state requires it.
6. **Cancel the insurance** once the car is sold or transferred, and ask for a refund of any unused
   premium.

It's okay to let the car sit for a while if no one is ready to decide. Just keep it insured and
safe.
