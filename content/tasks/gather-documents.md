You will be asked for many of the same things by different organizations. A single folder, physical
or digital, can save a lot of searching.

1. **Start with these:**
   - Certified copies of the death certificate
   - Their Social Security number
   - Their birth certificate and marriage certificate, if they were married
   - Their driver's license or ID
   - Military discharge papers (often called a DD 214), if they served
2. **Add money papers as you find them:**
   - Recent bank, credit card and investment statements
   - Insurance policies
   - Retirement and pension statements
   - Their last few tax returns
   - Property deeds, mortgage papers and car titles
3. **Keep your own papers close too.** You may need your ID and proof of your relationship, such as
   a birth or marriage certificate.
4. **Make copies or scans.** Many organizations will accept a copy for most things.

It's okay if you can't find everything. Most organizations can look things up with a name, date of
birth and address.
