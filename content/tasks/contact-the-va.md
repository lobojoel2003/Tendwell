Their service can be honored in meaningful ways. If you would like a burial in a national cemetery
or military honors at the funeral, tell the funeral home early so they can help arrange it.

1. **Find their discharge papers.** These are often called a DD 214. The funeral home or the VA can
   help you request a copy if you can't find them.
2. **Ask about burial benefits.** Depending on their service, these can include:
   - burial in a VA national cemetery
   - a headstone, marker or medallion
   - a burial flag and military funeral honors
   - a Presidential Memorial Certificate
   - help with burial costs, in some cases
3. **Ask about support for the family.** A surviving spouse or children may qualify for monthly
   payments, education benefits or health care.
4. **Get in touch.** You can call the VA at 1-800-827-1000, or ask a local veterans service
   organization for free help with the forms.

It's okay to ask for help with this. Veterans service officers help families with these
applications for free.
