A short phone call or email to their manager or human resources (HR) team is enough for now. They can
send the details in writing later.

1. **Let them know.** Their manager can tell colleagues, and HR can explain what happens next.
2. **Ask about money owed.** This can include a final paycheck, unused vacation pay, commissions or
   bonuses.
3. **Ask about benefits.** Many employers provide life insurance, and some offer accidental death
   cover or help with funeral costs. Ask who is named as the beneficiary.
4. **Ask about retirement plans.** A 401(k), 403(b) or pension usually goes to the beneficiary named
   on the account, not through the will.
5. **Ask about health insurance for the family.** If family members were on their plan, ask when the
   coverage ends and how to continue it. There is a separate step for this.
6. **Arrange to collect their belongings.** Someone at work can often pack up their desk or locker
   for you.

It's okay to ask HR to put everything in writing. You don't need to remember it all from one call.
