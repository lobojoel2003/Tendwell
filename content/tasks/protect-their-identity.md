This is a precaution, not an emergency. A few letters can greatly lower the chance of someone
misusing their identity.

1. **Tell the three credit bureaus.** Write to Equifax, Experian and TransUnion. Ask them to add a
   note to the file that the person has died and that no new credit should be issued. Include a copy
   of the death certificate and proof that you are the executor or a close relative.
2. **Ask for a copy of their credit report.** It can show accounts and debts you didn't know about.
3. **Add them to the deceased do-not-contact list.** The Association of National Advertisers runs a
   free list called DMAchoice that reduces marketing mail sent to someone who has died.
4. **Be careful what you share publicly.** Keep their full date of birth, home address and mother's
   maiden name out of obituaries and social media posts.
5. **Keep an eye on their mail.** Bills for accounts you don't recognize can be an early warning.

It's okay to send the same letter to all three bureaus. The template here works for each of them.
