This is often the most emotional part of the estate, because it's where the person's wishes become
real. Taking it slowly and keeping everyone informed helps.

1. **Wait until debts and taxes are settled.** If the executor gives money away too soon and the
   estate then can't pay its bills, the executor can be held responsible.
2. **Follow the will.** If there is no will, each state has rules, often called _intestacy_ rules,
   that say which relatives inherit and in what shares.
3. **Prepare a simple account.** Show what came into the estate, what was paid out and what is left.
   Share it with the beneficiaries before you distribute anything.
4. **Hand over gifts of specific items** as the will describes.
5. **Get a signed receipt** from each beneficiary for what they receive. Many courts expect this.
6. **Check whether the court needs to approve** the final distribution. In some states, it does.

It's okay to ask a lawyer to review the plan before money changes hands. A small fee now can prevent
disagreements later.
