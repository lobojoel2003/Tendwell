There's no right answer here. Some families keep a profile as a place to remember; others prefer to
close everything. You can decide one account at a time.

1. **Save what matters first.** Photos, videos and messages can be lost when an account is closed.
   Ask the family what they would like kept.
2. **Check for their wishes.** Some services let people choose in advance. Google has an Inactive
   Account Manager, Facebook lets people choose a legacy contact, and Apple has a Legacy Contact
   feature.
3. **Use each service's own process.** Most large services have a form for family members to
   memorialize or close an account. You'll usually need a death certificate.
4. **Think about email last.** Their email is often how you discover other accounts, and password
   resets go there.
5. **Be careful with passwords.** Even if you know their password, many services ask families not to
   sign in as the person. In most states, a law sets out what an executor can access.

It's okay to leave an account as it is for now. Many families come back to this months later.
