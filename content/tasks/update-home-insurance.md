A home is often the most valuable thing in an estate. Keeping it insured protects everyone.

1. **Call the home insurer.** Tell them about the death and ask:
   - how to keep the policy in force while the estate is settled
   - what the policy says about an empty home, and whether you need vacancy cover
   - who should be named on the policy now
2. **Keep paying the premiums** from the estate account, if you can.
3. **Tell the mortgage lender.** Ask about the balance and payments. Federal rules generally protect
   a family member who inherits a home from having the mortgage called due simply because of the
   death. The lender should treat them as a "successor in interest".
4. **Keep up with mortgage payments if possible,** while you decide whether to keep or sell the home.
5. **Check property taxes and any homeowners association fees.** These still need paying.
6. **Visit regularly,** or ask someone to, if no one lives there.

It's okay not to decide yet whether to keep or sell the home. Many families wait until the estate
is settled.
