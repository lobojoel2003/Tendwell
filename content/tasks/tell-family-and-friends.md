Telling people can be one of the hardest parts of the first days. It can help to share the load.

1. **Start with the people closest to them.** A short call is enough. You might say, "I have some
   very sad news. Mom died this morning."
2. **Ask for help spreading the news.** One person can contact a group of friends, another can tell
   work colleagues or a faith community. Their phone or address book can help you find people.
3. **Decide when to post online.** Some families wait until close relatives have been told directly.
   It's okay to ask others not to post anything until you're ready.
4. **Share funeral details later.** Once you know the date and place, you can send one message to
   everyone at once.
5. **Think about an obituary when you're ready.** The funeral home can help you write and publish
   one. Leave out details like their full date of birth and home address, which can be used for
   identity theft.

It's okay to let calls go to voicemail and reply by text. People understand.
