The funeral home usually files the death certificate with the state and can order certified copies
for you. If you are not using a funeral home, contact the vital records office in the state where
the death happened.

**How many?** Many families find 6 to 10 certified copies is enough. Some places accept a
photocopy, so it's worth asking before you send an original. You can always order more later.

1. Ask the funeral director to order copies, or ask how to order them yourself. Fees vary by state,
   and each extra copy usually costs less than the first.
2. Check the first copy carefully. Spelling mistakes and wrong dates are common, and they are much
   easier to fix early. The funeral home or vital records office can tell you how.
3. Keep one copy at home in a safe place.
4. Make a note of who you send copies to. If an organization doesn't need to keep it, ask them to
   send it back.

It's okay if the certificate takes a few weeks to arrive, especially if the cause of death is still
being confirmed. Many organizations will let you start the process and send the certificate later.
