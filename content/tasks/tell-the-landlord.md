Most landlords understand and will work with families. A short, honest conversation is a good start.

1. **Let them know about the death.** A phone call is fine, with a letter to follow so there's a
   record.
2. **Ask about the lease.** In many states, a lease doesn't end automatically when a tenant dies.
   Rent may still be owed by the estate until the lease ends or you agree to end it early. Rules vary
   by state, so ask what applies.
3. **Agree on a move-out date.** Ask for enough time to sort and clear their belongings.
4. **Ask about the security deposit.** It is usually returned to the estate once the home is handed
   back, minus any lawful deductions.
5. **If someone else lived there too,** ask whether they can stay and take over the lease.
6. **Return the keys** and ask for written confirmation that the tenancy has ended.

It's okay to ask for more time. Clearing a loved one's home is hard, and most landlords will
understand.
