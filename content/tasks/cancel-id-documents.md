This is a quiet, protective step. It rarely needs to happen quickly.

1. **Driver's license or state ID.** Contact your state's motor vehicle agency (often called the DMV).
   Some states ask you to return the license with a copy of the death certificate. Others update their
   records from the state's death records.
2. **Passport.** You can mail their passport and a copy of the death certificate to the U.S.
   Department of State to cancel it. Include a note if you would like the cancelled passport
   returned.
3. **Voter registration.** Many states remove people automatically using death records. If you want
   to be sure, contact the local election office.
4. **Professional licenses and memberships.** If they were a nurse, lawyer, contractor or similar,
   let the licensing board know.
5. **Disability parking placards, permits and other cards.** Return or cancel these as your state
   requires.

It's okay to keep a cancelled document. Many families treasure an old passport with its stamps.
