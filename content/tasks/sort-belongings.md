Sorting through someone's things can bring up a lot of memories, good and painful. It's often easier
to do in short sessions, with company.

1. **Check the will first.** It may give specific items to specific people.
2. **Start with the easy things.** Toiletries, food and everyday clothes can be a gentle way in.
3. **Make a fair way to choose keepsakes.** Some families take turns choosing one item at a time.
   Others put names on things they would like and talk through any overlaps.
4. **Take photos.** A photo can hold the memory of something you can't keep.
5. **Keep important papers.** Set aside anything to do with money, property, taxes or the estate
   until the executor says it can go. Shred papers with personal details before throwing them away.
6. **Find good homes for the rest.** Charities, friends, community groups and estate sale companies
   can help.

It's okay to keep a box of things you can't decide about yet, and to come back to it in a year.
