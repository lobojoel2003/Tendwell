For the next few months, their mail will tell you a lot: which banks they used, which subscriptions
are still running and which bills need paying.

1. **If someone still lives at the address**, they can simply keep collecting the mail.
2. **If the home is empty**, ask a neighbor or family member to collect it regularly.
3. **To forward it to you**, the person handling the estate can usually ask the post office to
   redirect it. Ask at a local post office what proof they need. This is often a death certificate
   and papers showing you are the executor.
4. **Note every new sender.** Add any accounts you find to the Accounts list in your plan.
5. **Reduce junk mail.** Adding them to the DMAchoice deceased do-not-contact list cuts down on
   marketing mail over a few months.

It's okay if opening their mail feels hard. Someone else in the family could take this on, or you
could open it together.
