This is the final piece of the paperwork. For many families, it comes somewhere between several
months and a couple of years after the death.

1. **Make sure everything is done.** Debts and taxes paid, the final tax returns filed, assets
   distributed and receipts collected.
2. **Prepare a final account,** if the court or the will requires one. It shows everything that came
   in and went out.
3. **File the closing papers** with the probate court, if the estate went through probate. The court
   can tell you which forms it needs.
4. **Close the estate bank account** once the last payments have cleared.
5. **Keep the records.** Keep the estate's papers for a few years after closing in case of questions.
   An accountant can tell you how long.
6. **Let the beneficiaries know** that the estate is closed.

It's okay to feel a mix of relief and sadness when this is done. Finishing the paperwork can bring
the loss back into focus.
